## 2. About (max 2,600 characters)

I build lending products where machine learning, product design and growth strategy are the same job.

I care most about credit that grows with the customer: transparent limits, prices that reflect real capacity, and product surfaces that make the path to more credit obvious. Outside work: running, writing, cooking, and venture philanthropy with Social Venture Partners Dallas.
