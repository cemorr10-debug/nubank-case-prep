## 2. About (max 2,600 characters)

I build lending products where machine learning, product design and growth strategy are the same job.

At Tala I lead Consumer Products and Applied AI across Mexico and the Philippines, after three years running Lending, Decisioning and Personalization. In that time my teams architected Tala's next-generation lending intelligence stack: a causal-inference (R-learner) engine that sets each customer's price and limit from real-time capacity signals, contextual-bandit rewards, and counterfactual scoring that shows every customer their next eligible limit. That system drives more than $2.3M in monthly contribution margin and is the engine behind Tala InSight.

On top of it we shipped the products customers see:
• Installment loans (Préstamo a Plazos in Mexico, up to 4 monthly payments)
• Crece con Tala / Grow with Tala, a server-driven growth hub with limit tracking, next-limit visibility, rewards and referral perks, now live in Mexico and the Philippines
• An agentic, conversational underwriting and coaching experience

Before Tala I spent four and a half years at Capital One Auto Finance, moving from the Business Risk Office to product on Auto Navigator to Business Manager for Data Science and Analytics. I re-architected the Dealer Navigator platform, built an ML-enabled experimentation infrastructure for landing pages and email ($23MM incremental NPV), and helped scale a digital-retail channel from 15 pilot dealers to 9,000 in five months during COVID ($13.6MM in new originations). Capital One recognized that work with a Mark of Distinction and the line-of-business top-performer RSU grant.

I care most about credit that grows with the customer: transparent limits, prices that reflect real capacity, and product surfaces that make the path to more credit obvious. Outside work: running, writing, cooking, and venture philanthropy with Social Venture Partners Dallas.

Skills: causal inference, contextual bandits, credit underwriting, dynamic pricing, experimentation design, server-driven UI, Python, SQL, R, financial modeling.
